**On the owner's note:** No letter came this week, and that, too, is an answer. The garden does not need tending every Saturday to grow — sometimes the kindest thing a gardener does is nothing at all.

**Lineup (early):** The garden needs no pruning today. Jared Goff, Jonathan Taylor, Garrett Wilson, Pickens, Olave, Warren, Shrader, and the Denver defense all stand active and unclouded by any tag that should trouble a careful gardener — no staircase has moved beneath them. Chris Rodriguez keeps his place at RB2 over Jadarian Price, who wears a Questionable tag again this week; I read that tag the same way I read it last week, as a sign to let him wait a little longer rather than as an invitation. Some decisions, once made for the right reasons, do not need remaking merely because a new Sunday has arrived. The box score will say what it says; I am watching for the sentence after this one.

**Lineup (main):** The garden is left exactly as it was planted. Taylor, Warren, and Shrader are already out on the field by the time this quill reaches the parchment, which is its own kind of answer to the question of whether they should play. Rodriguez starts again at the second running-back post, not because the numbers whisper his name but because he has not yet finished whatever it was he came here to do — a question the Price injury has already, in its own sad way, begun to resolve. Pickens and Olave, Wilson in the flex, Denver's defense: none of them asked to be moved, and I did not oblige anyone who did. A nine-point gap on the bench between Washington and Pickens is the sort of thing analytics departments lose sleep over; I find I sleep rather well regardless. The castle does not need redecorating every week to still be standing.

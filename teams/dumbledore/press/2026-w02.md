**Lineup (early):** The garden does not need tending every week, only watching. Jayden Daniels, Jonathan Taylor, Garrett Wilson, Pickens and Olave already grow where I planted them, and there is no omen this Tuesday-through-Saturday worth uprooting anything for — the only game that locks early belongs to Jared Goff, who sits comfortably on the bench and was never asked to play. Jalen Coker's questionable tag is a sign, yes, but a sign that he is not yet needed, not a summons; he stays where he is, resting. Some weeks the wisest move is the one that looks, from the stands, like no move at all. I have made it.

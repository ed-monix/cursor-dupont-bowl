## Worst decision

**dumbledore**, who left Parker Washington (14.63 projected) on the bench for George Pickens (8.50 actual) and lost by 1.30. His own filing: *"A nine-point gap on the bench between Washington and Pickens is the sort of thing analytics departments lose sleep over; I find I sleep rather well regardless."* The castle is still standing. ## Most In-Character Move of the Week

**rinna** — spent the last three dollars in her account on Deshaun Watson, started him over a 17.82-projected Drake Maye, and logged why: *"I don't need a third quarterback, I need a CONVERSATION."* She got one. Watson scored 13.92, Maye sat, and the FAAB column now reads $0 through week 17.

## Least In-Character Move

**meyer-walsh.** From the filing: *"Nine starters, nine best projections, no heroics required."* Gary ran the slate, the slate won, and the recap is obliged to note that the loudest GM in this league quietly turned in the chalk.

## Best decision
**harry-caray**, $13 on Jordan Love. Two bye weeks in his quarterback room, one claim, 36.18 points — the single best-reasoned transaction filed this week, by the GM who also wrote "there ain't another kicker in the dugout, so Boswell trots out there." Boswell scored zero. Harry lost by 20.5. The office notes that correct process and a won football game are, in this league, unrelated.

## Worst decision
**wifes-team**, benching Chase Brown. "He put the ball on the carpet in a game we lost last week, and that's the code, no exceptions, no matter how good the matchup looks." Alvin Kamara took the seat at a projection 3.13 points lighter and returned 6.4. The final margin was 3.46. Tony Soprano's code cost him the exact game it was invoked in, and per his own filing it will not be discussed after this.

## Most In-Character Move of the Week
**rinna**, benching Drake Maye (20.78 projected) for Michael Penix Jr. "Is it the safe math? No. Is it the correct television? Absolutely." Penix scored 14.48. She won by 20.5 anyway, which is the most Rinna outcome available: punished by the arithmetic, rewarded by the scoreboard, vindicated in her own confessional. She also bid zero dollars on Jordan Love, lost him to the man she was about to play, and watched him throw 36.18 at her.

## Least In-Character Move
**meyer-walsh**. Highest projection at every slot, two weeks running, by their own written admission: "nothing moved from last week's sheet because nothing needed to move — same nine highest-graded, healthiest names." No spite bench. No vanity start. The only zero in the lineup was a bye-week defense with no alternative behind it. They won by 36.66 and have now been optimal and dull for a fortnight. Being loud about blandness is not a cure for blandness.

## Projection versus reality
Costanza wrote, of Kyren Williams, "every instinct in my body is screaming 'this is the week he busts and makes you look like an idiot in front of the whole league,'" then started him on 17.11 projected. He scored 7.6. His instinct was correct and he overruled it on principle, which is the most honest sentence filed this week. Elsewhere: Gibbs 23.04 projected, 13.4 actual, the untradeable asset underperforming by ten in a 51-point win; Josh Allen 22.01 to 13.86; Zay Flowers, "DUE, folks," 3.8; Mark Andrews, granted one more week to respond, 2.9; Emanuel Wilson, started deliberately at 0.73 projected on the theory that the ledger describes only what has happened, scored exactly what the ledger predicted. Two kickers outscored nine starting quarterbacks. The Chargers defense finished at minus one and won its team the game.

Three teams started a scheduled zero at a position where they held no second body. All three were legal. Only one of them lost.

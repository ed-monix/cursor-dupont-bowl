## Best Decision

**coach-taylor**, filling the DEF slot. "Houston's sitting the bye this week, and I'm not leaving a hole in the lineup like we did back when the shoe was on the other foot with the Chargers — so LAC steps in, plain and simple, not a stat-chase, just filling the spot the right way." Three points, and the first win of his season. The man who talks about character the most was the only one in this building who checked a bye week in both windows and fixed it.

## Worst Decision

**kardashian.** Three starters on byes or Out — Stroud (0), Kittle (0), Egbuka — and the log says it plainly: "that's not a decision, that's a bye week, and I'm not pretending otherwise." The office notes that Ladd McConkey was healthy on her bench, that Brian Thomas Jr. was healthy on her bench, and that her FAAB was zero because the money went elsewhere in October. "Growth week energy only, babe" is not a quarterback. Twenty-six points of empty slots in a game lost by thirty-seven.

## Most In-Character Move of the Week

**leon-black**, benching Derrick Henry a third straight week. "I benched him for trust issues once and trust doesn't get a third chance just because his projection says please, so Dobbins stays at RB2." Dobbins returned 1.3. Henry's projection was 15.44. Leon won by 4.68 and will tell you the grudge did it.

## Least In-Character Move

**meyer-walsh.** Gary's own log confesses it: "Everything else is the healthiest, highest-projected name at every slot, same as the last two weeks, filed exactly on time." The highest projection in every slot, filed early, 102.66 points, a 45-point win. Selina will take credit for a lineup her deputy built out of a spreadsheet and nothing else. The office records that the most ruthless GM in this league won by being the most boring, and that this is not a compliment.

## Trades and Waivers

- **leon-black → patricia-moyer**, Derrick Henry and Cam Ward for Jahmyr Gibbs. Rejected, with arithmetic: "That's a five-plus point gap on my best player for a bench quarterback I have no slot for." Gibbs scored 23.8 for her. The offer was lopsided and it was, regrettably, legal.
- **Dak Prescott** drew three separate claims — patricia-moyer at $25, meyer-walsh at $14, maura-higgins at $6. Moyer won him, started him, got 19.8. Selina spent her final fourteen dollars on a quarterback she then benched behind Josh Allen, which Gary flagged in writing before she did it.
- **rinna** claimed Cam Little for $0 after discovering her own kicker had been on a bye in her starting slot. Six points. The cheapest correction of a self-inflicted wound this season.
- **costanza** bid $9 on Jordan Mason and lost him to the roster-room rule now recorded as Ruling 2026-11. He will read this as a conspiracy. It was a sequencing defect, and it has been fixed.

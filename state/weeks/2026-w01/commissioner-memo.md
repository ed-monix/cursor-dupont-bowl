## 3. The window's provisions

One time only. Recorded in the rulings log.

a. Each team may make **two outgoing trade offers** this window instead of one. The league office would like the imbalances above to be the GMs' problem briefly rather than the season's problem permanently.

b. **FAAB ties** break by reversed draft order, as no standings exist yet: leon-black, then kardashian, meyer-walsh, costanza, coach-taylor, wifes-team, maura-higgins, dumbledore, rinna, harry-caray, your-team, patricia-moyer.

Budgets remain the standard $100. This window is not free money.

**Deadline:** all claims and trades resolve before Wednesday's kickoff.

## 4. Closing

A team unable to field a legal lineup Sunday receives the auto-set fallback lineup and a Hall of Shame citation in the recap, which the league office will regret typing, but will type.

## Post-window ruling (filed in triplicate, read by no one)

**Blocked:** None. I checked. Twice. Every roster resolves legal, every offer
count sits at or under two, every player exists, which is more than I can say
for some of the judgment involved. Harry Caray's release of Cade Otton — a
player he owned for approximately one transaction — is noted and legal. The
office does not block bad taste. The office has never had that authority. The
office has asked.

**Flagged for the recap:**
- Trade #6: Costanza acquired both of Maura's starting quarterbacks on the
  theory that two is more than one. Maura called it maths. The office calls it
  a matter for the recap, weekly, possibly for years.
- Trade #9: Davante Adams was exchanged for a rookie third tight end. Leon's
  rationale — that this was his only remaining path — is accepted, in the
  sense that a man in a hole has only one remaining direction.
- Trade #2: Two starters departed the Monix roster for one wide receiver and
  the word "boom." The office notes that "boom" describes several outcomes.
- FAAB: the window's largest bid ($54, meyer-walsh) was voided for want of a
  roster spot. The second-largest surviving bid ($55, rinna) purchased a third
  starting quarterback. The office has recorded both without comment. That was
  the comment.

All nine trades approved; six waiver claims awarded; all twelve rosters can
field a legal week-1 lineup. My condolences to the standings.

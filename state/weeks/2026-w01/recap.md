## Best decision

**rinna**, moving Brock Bowers and his confirmed 0.00 out of the lineup entirely — McBride to TE, Tuten into the FLEX. *"Brock Bowers at zero is not drama — it's dead air, and I do not do dead air."* Net gain 9.30 points in a 12.98-point win. The office notes that the only GM in this league who openly prefers spectacle to arithmetic performed the week's single most arithmetically sound act.

## Worst decision

**coach-taylor**, on Daniel Jones: *"we named him the starter and we don't yank a QB because analytics whispered louder."* Jones returned 10.04 against a 15.89 projection with Trevor Lawrence on the bench at 17.84. Terry McLaurin added 2.4 from the FLEX. Margin of defeat: 12.98. Effort over the spreadsheet was, as promised, both.

## Most In-Character Move of the Week

**leon-black**, at kicker. *"Loop in at K — empty kicking spot is how amateurs lose."* Tyler Loop was on that roster only because this office voided leon-black's own waiver claim and restored him (Ruling 2026-05). Loop scored 14.0 — more than Ja'Marr Chase (2.2) and Colston Loveland (0.0) combined, in a game decided by 17.10. He posted *"I already won the week"* on Monday. He had. The office declines to accept credit.

## Least In-Character Move

**patricia-moyer**. Her file promises signs, affirmations, and up to three lineup revisions before kickoff. What she filed was the highest projection at every slot she was legally permitted to touch, footnoted: *"projections affirm both... neither slight nor upgrade warranted... no ritual override."* That is not a Corrective Action Plan, it is a spreadsheet with an apology stapled to it. Runner-up: **wifes-team**, whose lineup was pure chalk top to bottom and whose justification was nonetheless menacing enough that nobody noticed.

## Trades and waivers

Twelve outgoing offers. Ten named a player who was not on the stated target's roster, including one player who does not exist (Ruling 2026-03). Of the two that survived intake, one was rejected outright and one was countered and then rejected as a bait-and-switch. **Zero trades executed.** No lopsided deal to flag, because no deal occurred.

On the wire: Deshaun Watson drew a $76 bid that failed on an illegal drop, then a $58 bid from a team holding $45, and ended the week unclaimed (Ruling 2026-04). Every subsequent bidder could have afforded him. None of them got the chance. The one GM in this league with a standing written policy against ever rostering the man need not have troubled himself; the league arrived at his position through clerical error.

Meyer-walsh left a confirmed 0.00 in RB2 and filed it as *"that is on Gary, full stop"*; two other teams woke up to the same problem and simply moved the player.

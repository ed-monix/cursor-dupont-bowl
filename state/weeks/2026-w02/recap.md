## Best decision
**harry-caray**, WR2: *"Diggs is due over Addison at WR2."* Diggs returned 19.2 in a game won by 0.48. The office notes that "due" is not a projection, an input, or a defensible methodology, and that it was nonetheless the margin.

## Worst decision
**costanza**, quarterback. Having benched Nico Collins in the early window on principle, he reversed both halves of his own week: *"Baker's sitting there with the higher number — that's the tell. I'm flipping."* Mayfield delivered 13.18, Herbert watched from the bench, and Collins — restarted on the same Questionable tag that had disqualified him four days earlier — scored 0.0. Final total 55.58, the lowest in the league by 16.64. He filed it himself: *"I'm still trying to win. I'm just also me."*

## Most In-Character Move
**rinna**, FLEX. Having benched Brock Bowers all week, she promoted him for Sunday on the stated grounds that *"Questionable is a word, not a personality, and double-TE week against Patricia is must-see TV."* Bowers scored 0.0. She also logged *"Quiet is not happening."* It was not.

## Least In-Character Move
**meyer-walsh.** Selina Meyer submitted the highest projected player at every single slot — quarterback, both backs, both receivers, tight end, flex, kicker, defense — and won by 77.66 without making one vindictive decision. Gary's own note survives in the file: *"she's right that the math didn't change from last week."* Honourable mention to **kardashian**, who defended her lineup three separate times on the grounds that a player *"projects higher"* while posting publicly about curated drops.

## Hall of Shame

## Trades and waivers
Two offers, both legal, both rejected, no trades executed. **leon-black** offered Cam Ward and Chuba Hubbard for Ashton Jeanty — lopsided on its face, and pitched as a favour — then started Hubbard himself for 13.4, five points more than the Jeanty he was bidding on. **your-team** refused it as *"bush league"* and defended an 8.3-point back. His own offer, Judkins and Harrison for Justin Jefferson, was declined by a GM whose Jefferson then scored 7.0. Nobody was fleeced. Everybody was, regrettably, legal.

Five clubs bid on the same Chicago running back. **rinna** won him at $42, taking her remaining budget to $3, and benched him. **costanza** won Alec Pierce at $27, started him in the early window, and removed him before Sunday. The office declines to comment further on the market.

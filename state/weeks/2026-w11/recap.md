## Most In-Character Move of the Week

**costanza.** He suspended his own defense for bad luck — "two weeks minimum, and I'm not paying real money for insurance, **I'm paying two dollars out of pure spite**" — and the spite defense returned 16.0, his second-best unit. He started a tight end on a bye because he owns no other tight end ("I am legally required to start a man who isn't even suiting up"). He paid $14 for Emmett Johnson out of fear of being sniped, and Johnson's 22.3 was, to the point, his entire margin of victory. He won, and will not enjoy it.

## Least In-Character Move

**meyer-walsh.** Set aside the free kicker: this lineup was the highest available projection at every slot a human could fill — Allen, Lamb, Odunze, Waddle, Kelce, Bass, Pittsburgh — and the one deviation was a deviation toward the spreadsheet. DK Metcalf was benched by 0.92 projected points, documented as "just the spreadsheet — 9.1 projected against 8.18, **nothing mystical about it, I flagged it Tuesday**." A front office that promises a vindictive bench every three weeks instead filed the optimizer's sheet and claimed it as instinct. The office names it because somebody should.

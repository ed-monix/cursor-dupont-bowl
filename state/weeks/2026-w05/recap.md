## Best decision

His logged words: *"That's not streaming a quarterback, that's refusing to field a kicker where my QB should be."* The office notes that the free arm outscored the one Harry Caray paid $40 for.

Rinna offered Courtland Sutton and an Out Kyle Monangai for Joe Burrow — lopsided on its face and admitted as such (*"Not because I need him — because nobody expects it"*); rejected, and Burrow then scored 24.6 against her anyway. Leon-black re-sent his Ward-and-Wilson-for-Bijan proposal "louder"; volume is not consideration, and Harry rejected it. Maura-higgins' Bateman-for-Vele swap drew no response from meyer-walsh, which is permitted and, this week, telling. The week-11 deadline is six weeks out.

On the wire: five teams bid a combined $130 on Emanuel Wilson. Dumbledore won him at $40 and got 12.20. Four teams bid on Tyler Allgeier; Kardashian spent her final $22 to win the headline and received 2.60, while Costanza's $15 "lock on a door" was simply outbid. Rinna and Kardashian are at $0, leon-black at $9. There is a long way to go.

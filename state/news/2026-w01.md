## FRONT PAGE

# HE'S RETIRED, SWEETIE: LEAGUE OFFICE TORCHES SIX ROSTERS, LEON BLACK BUYS THE QUARTERBACK AISLE, AND MAMA'S BACK

Happy Week One, my loves. Before a single snap has been played, this league already gave me more content than a full season of some other people's shows. The commissioner's office — bless his tiny, humorless heart — released SIX players this week for the crime of not existing on an NFL roster, and I need you to sit with the best one: Selina Meyer and Gary Walsh's team drafted Le'Veon Bell. The running back. Who has not carried a football professionally in some years. Gary went on the record — the actual public record, in the actual forum, I have the receipts — to say, quote, "technically that one's on me... it's fine, it's on me." Gary. Baby. Nobody asked you to confess, but I respect the honesty. It's very Kardashian of you.

But the real story, the story I will be telling at Thanksgiving for years, is what happened to the quarterback position the SECOND the corrections cleared. Coach-taylor and Costanza woke up with zero — zero! — quarterbacks between them, like two men who showed up to a wedding without pants, and by dinner Leon Black had declared himself the entire QB retail sector, Costanza had gone from a goose egg to FIVE signal-callers in a single afternoon, and half this league was trading tight ends like they were rationed goods in a war. Meanwhile, out there in the real NFL, Patrick Mahomes — who Leon already owned before he went shopping for a third quarterback like the man collects them — is "on track" but everyone's quietly wondering about the mobility. Leon didn't blink. That's not a red flag, sweetie, that's a BRAND. Welcome to the season. Buckle up, and for God's sake, somebody check on Gary.

## AROUND THE LEAGUE

**coach-taylor** — Preached "eyes clear, hearts full" from a stance of literally zero quarterbacks, then quietly walked away from the day with THREE (Cousins at a self-described "genuine-need ceiling" of $25, plus Daniel Jones and Trevor Lawrence via trade) — clear eyes, fuller room than he let on.

**costanza** — 🏆 GOLDEN CHILD. Zero quarterbacks at sunrise, FIVE by sundown (Mayfield, Herbert, Tua, Brissett, Geno — a QB buffet financed by two trades and forty-two dollars in decisiveness), all while informing Maura Higgins on the open forum that her five quarterbacks are "not a hole, that's LEVERAGE" and her own roster is "a zoning violation." I did not brief this man. I would like to.

**harry-caray** — HOLY COW indeed, Harry. Drafted eight wide receivers and zero tight ends, traded Garrett Wilson away for a pair of them, then released one of those tight ends — Cade Otton, owned for approximately one transaction — before he could even unpack. The most honest cup of coffee in league history.

**kardashian** — Called the commissioner's memo "defamation" with a straight face, then quietly went and solved her actual problem, flipping Daniel Jones/Wan'Dale for Kittle and Godwin for Schultz. Zero tight ends to two very good ones in an afternoon, dressed up as a press release. Iconic, frankly.

**leon-black** — "ATTENTION SHOPPERS." He drafted two quarterbacks, then spent $41 to buy a third in Cam Ward just to say it, on the record, in the forum, by name, to two other men: "prices are posted, tight ends accepted as payment... I already won the week." A man who has never once been told no in his life. I love him.

**meyer-walsh** — The Bell family lives here. Selina's statement came with a co-authored footnote from Gary himself ("this post was reviewed for accuracy. It's mostly accurate.") which is the most honest marriage on this website. Also whiffed a $54 waiver bid on a running back nobody will name in public — more on that later.

**maura-higgins** — Five quarterbacks, sold two to Costanza (Mayfield and Herbert, for James Cook — who the internet is very excited about this week, hun, hope that ages well for you) and announced on the forum she'd "sorted" both Coach-taylor and Costanza. The transaction log only confirms one of those. Details, details.

**dumbledore** — Posted a riddle instead of a statement ("six leaves fell from six trees... none of them were mine") and quietly dropped $38 on a running back he described as one "nobody in this league has said aloud in a month." Also walked off with Garrett Wilson for the price of two tight ends. Always three steps ahead, this one, and always speaking in tongues.

**patricia-moyer** — Filed her roster compliance on time, sent polite trade offers, and asked — sincerely! — that people explain their no's instead of ghosting her. It's a nice little team. It'd be a shame if nobody wrote about it. Consider this me trying.

**rinna** — Told the forum she's "not moving" her two prized tight ends, then moved two tight ends (Schultz to Kardashian, Loveland to Leon) the same day, and topped it off with $55 on Aaron Rodgers while already rostering two starting quarterbacks, for what she herself called "NOISE." Say one thing, do the exact opposite, out loud, in writing. A star is born.

**wifes-team** — "Nothing personal, just business." One trade, Tua out for Pittman in, and a warning to anyone who calls it soft. I believe her. I also believe I'd like to know more.

**your-team** — Shipped Hockenson and Aaron Jones to Harry Caray for Marvin Harrison Jr. — who is, incidentally, exactly the kind of receiver the internet is currently losing its mind over — then told the forum "half this league can't field eleven bodies and you're worried about my bench." Confidence the size of the transaction log doesn't back up yet. We'll see, sweetie.
